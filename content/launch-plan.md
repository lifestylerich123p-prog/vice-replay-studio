# Vice Replay content plan

The daily automation picks the row for today's date (UTC). If the facts for that topic aren't out yet,
or another creator angle clearly fits the day better, it picks a better topic and logs why.
Every day also gets a standalone news Short, ideally about what big streamers and creators are
doing with GTA 6 (launch marathons, first reactions, records, viral moments).
After the last row, it picks trending GTA 6 topics itself (see `.claude/commands/daily-video.md`).

## Rules
- Launch day and after: avoid story spoilers in titles, thumbnails and the first 30 seconds.
- Only report things that verifiable sources say. Label leaks and rumors as such.
- Tips/money/hidden-detail videos: base them on what's reported by reputable sites and players (and cite
  them), never invented game mechanics.

## Evergreen gear guides (affiliate; people search these for months)
Slot one in whenever the day has no big news, before Black Friday if possible. Use `affiliate: ["storage","gear"]`.
| Main video | Short |
|---|---|
| Best SSD for GTA 6 on PS5 (what to look for: 2TB, heatsink, speed) | "GTA 6 is 212GB. Here's the fix." |
| GTA 6 storage full? Free up space on PS5 and Xbox (free fixes first, then upgrades) | "Delete this before GTA 6" |
| Best settings + gear for GTA 6 (controller, headset, TV game mode) | "3 settings to change in GTA 6" |
Be honest: free fixes first, upgrades second; never claim we tested a product.
