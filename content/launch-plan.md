# Vice Replay content plan

The daily automation picks the row for today's date (UTC). If the facts for that topic aren't out yet,
or another creator angle clearly fits the day better, it picks a better topic and logs why.
After the last row, it picks trending GTA 6 topics itself (see `.claude/commands/daily-video.md`).

| Date (2026) | Main video | Short |
|---|---|---|
| Tue Nov 17 | GTA 6 Unlock Time in Every Time Zone (US, UK, EU, Australia) | "GTA 6 unlocks at midnight... but which midnight?" |
| Wed Nov 18 | GTA 6 Reviews Are In: What Critics Are Saying (only if reviews are out; else: Leonida map, everything we know) | The one-sentence verdict from the biggest reviews |
| Thu Nov 19 | GTA 6 IS OUT: Launch Day Survival Guide (patch, servers, settings to change first) | First 3 settings to change in GTA 6 |
| Fri Nov 20 | GTA 6 Beginner Tips Nobody Tells You | 1 tip that saves you hours |
| Sat Nov 21 | Best Early Money Methods in GTA 6 | Fastest money in the first hour |
| Sun Nov 22 | Wildest Moments From GTA 6's First Weekend (what players are sharing) | The single wildest clip story of the week |
| Mon Nov 23 | GTA 6 Hidden Details Players Found in Week One | One hidden detail, explained |

## Rules
- Launch day and after: avoid story spoilers in titles, thumbnails and the first 30 seconds.
- Only report things that verifiable sources say. Label leaks and rumors as such.
- Tips/money/hidden-detail videos: base them on what's reported by reputable sites and players (and cite
  them), never invented game mechanics.
