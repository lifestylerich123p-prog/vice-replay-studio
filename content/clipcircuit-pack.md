## 2. Check freshness before every clip (the rule that saves the most views)
A moment many clippers already posted gets ~1 view (duplicate suppression). Lacy's shark clip got 1 view; the
less-posted shoe store got 7K+. Before cutting a moment:
1. Search YouTube Shorts for "<streamer> <moment>" from the last 12 hours.
2. If 3+ clippers already posted it, skip it. Pick a moment with 0-1 copies.
3. Vice Replay runs this automatically for GTA 6 streamers (`python -m studio.moments <name>`) and can share the
   "TAKEN" list with you on request.

## 3. What gets views (data, Nov 18-20)
- **Length:** in 307 Lacy GTA 6 Shorts, the top 20% by views/hour had a median of 14 s (rest 24 s) and got ~1,350
  views/hour vs ~190. 8-16 s clips got ~7x the views/hour of 20-30 s ones. Short, one idea, payoff fast.
- **Titles:** name first + the moment, simple: "Lacy Arrested on Day 1 in GTA 6!! ".
- **Format winners this week (top 432 GTA 6 Shorts):** GTA 5 vs GTA 6 comparisons ~940K typical, streamer
  moments ~760K, real gameplay ~520K, memes ~400K, news ~290K. 127 of the 432 came from channels under 20K subs.
- **Money math:** pool campaigns need 750K-1M approved views to share (long shot when hundreds of clippers post);
  per-view campaigns ($1-2 per 1K, $3 minimum) pay from the first few thousand views. Vendor data: clippers
  average ~$0.39 per 1K after fees. Prefer per-view campaigns.

## 4. Rules that protect reach (YouTube + Instagram)
- **Originality (YouTube, Oct 2026):** clips need real added value: a hook card, captions, a context line, tight
  edit. Never re-upload an untouched clip; never post the same clip twice; never delete and re-upload (spam signal).
- **Repetitive content (YouTube, July 2026):** vary hook cards/caption styles across uploads; don't let every
  clip look identical. Max 2 campaign clips a day per account.
- **Instagram:** accounts whose posts are mostly reposts lose recommendations; go slow on new accounts (Meta flagged
  @vice.replaytv's ads after 11 API posts in one day): max 4 posts/day, ~2.5 h apart.
- **Campaign rules always win:** Lacy: no logos of any kind, Lacy the main focus, LACY in caption + #LACY, never
  portray him negatively, no AI-generated content, English captions, submit within 30 minutes.

## 5. A simple scorecard (do this daily)
For each posted clip, log views at ~24 h in the inventory with: campaign, length (s), hook type, platform, moment
freshness (copies found), posting hour. Every 3 days compare medians by length, platform and freshness, and do more
of what wins. (Vice Replay's version: `content/learn/scorecard.md` in its repo.)
