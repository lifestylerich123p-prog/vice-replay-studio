# Growth plan from the Nov 22, 2026 investigation

Three web research passes (Shorts algorithm and policy, what works for GTA 6 channels, Shorts tactics) plus our own
YouTube Analytics. Search snippets only (most article hosts are blocked here); confidence noted.

## What the evidence says
1. **Originality is the main risk** (strong). YouTube's Oct 1, 2026 Shorts update cuts recommendations for channels that
   mostly re-use footage "without significant changes"; YouTube's Creator Liaison said added value must be "not just VO
   descriptions of what's happening on screen". Reused-content rules apply even with permission (so Rockstar's fan policy
   doesn't settle it) and are judged channel-wide. July 2025/2026 "inauthentic content" rules target template-based,
   mass-produced uploads; an AI voice alone isn't disqualifying. Sources: searchenginejournal.com/youtube-original-shorts-
   reposted-clips-reach/591774/, support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392, tubefilter.com/2026/07/13/youtube-inauthentic-
   content-monetization-policy-update/.
2. **Demand moved to gameplay** (strong): 125.5M hours of GTA VI streams in 24 h; tiny channels still break out with one
   specific angle (Meda, ~1.2K subs, 1.5M views on one Short). cnbc.com/2026/11/20/gta-vi-launch-streaming-content-economy.html
3. **Short beats long for Shorts** (medium): 11-20 s had the best median views in a 108K-Short study (quso.ai/research/
   youtube-shorts-length). Ours: real moments held 86-92% at 13-17 s; ~30 s explainers ~57%.
4. **Seed test** (strong for the mechanism): each Short goes to a seed audience first; no official pass mark, so compare
   with our own median. Shorts get little after ~30 days (medium).
5. **No new-channel penalty** (strong for "no evidence of one"): low early views don't doom the channel; looking like an
   aggregator does.
6. **Don't delete flops** (strong, YouTube's Todd Beaupré): unlist at most.
7. **Spanish** (weak-medium): a separate Spanish channel beat an extra audio track 3,897 vs 32 views in one test (Kapwing);
   IlloJuan (403.7K) and Rubius (375.0K) were the biggest Spanish launch streams (preliminary, streamer.guide).
8. **Human element** (weak): one outlet says faceless channels are disadvantaged; viewers trust human voices more (Adobe
   survey). Worth a test, not a rule.

## Our numbers (YouTube Analytics, Nov 13-20)
- Stayed to watch (engaged views / views): median 40%. Best openers 50-55% ("30 FPS EVERYWHERE", "$1.65 BILLION", "Already
  out in NZ"); worst 29% ("SPEED TO NZ", "STREAMERS GOT BOXES").
- 1.5 likes per 100 views; 0.6 subscribers per 1,000 views.

## Changes made (system)
- Views check blocks Shorts over ~30 s and warns over 20 s; daily run: max 2 narrated Shorts/day, 13-20 s, number/catch
  first, commentary not description, formats in order (streamers, community discoveries on the map, quiz/map, big news).
- Retention report shows "stayed to watch" per Short vs our median.

## Owner actions (only the owner can do these)
1. **Gameplay** (biggest lever by far): PS5 Create button -> Save recent gameplay -> 30 s, to the Drive drop zone. Even 3-5
   clips a day turn every Short original.
2. **Voice test**: record 4 short scripts in his own voice (phone voice memo, quiet room) vs 4 Kokoro versions.
3. **Collabs**: YouTube Shorts Collaborations (up to 5 co-creators, shown to each audience) with 1-2 small GTA 6 creators.
4. Publish the Google app (keys expire Tuesday); pin comments; set Related video on every Short.
