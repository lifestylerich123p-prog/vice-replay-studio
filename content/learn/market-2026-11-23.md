## Card / lettering Shorts (owner Nov 23: no trailer images; "lettering and more modern style, GTA 6 style")
Web (FluxNote, Influencers-Time, AIR Media-Tech, YouTube's blog on tier lists, synthwave design sources) + plugins:
- Text on screen is the norm for faceless Shorts; kinetic type works when it animates only the KEY words, in time
  with the voice; too much motion slows the video down. Change the picture every 2-4 s (8-12 scenes per 30 s).
- Formats that drive comments: tier lists (gaming-born; S = "super"), countdown listicles (4-6 s per item),
  quizzes with a visible timer, VS / "this or that". Chat-story Shorts retain well but are fiction: not for a facts
  channel.
- Design: synthwave itself comes from GTA: Vice City (Wikipedia, citing the shift to '80s homage); neon glow + drop
  shadow keep lettering legible; two fonts max, one idea per card, key phrase first (quote-cards skill).
- Plugins: vidIQ `motion_graphics` renders kinetic text / stat counters / VS cards (1 credit per 4 s) but vidIQ has
  0 credits until Dec 15. Everygen has 56 animated "video kits" for facts Shorts (stat, countdown, gauge, timer,
  phone) as style references, and a free SFX library; our own renderer covers the same ground for free.
Built (Nov 23): studio/vicecards.py (Vice City set + kinetic, countdown, versus, tier, headline), the whole hook on
the first frame (cover), soft pops where words land; first Shorts: gta6-tier-list-critics, gta6-4-secrets (restyled).
Next to add: a gauge/meter card ("hype meter", % of players), a timer card for quizzes, and Everygen's Viral Adam
