# Competitor research: GTA 6 Shorts channels, EN + ES (2026-11-26)

**Method.** YouTube Data API through `studio.learn.call` (~870 of 1,500 units): 8 searches (Shorts length, published since
Nov 12, sorted by views, 4 English + 4 Spanish queries: location / where to find / secret easter egg / map; ubicación /
dónde está / cómo conseguir / mapa secretos), then channel stats + the last 50 uploads of 21 channels and the top 10
comments of 20 videos. Spanish demand: Google + YouTube autocomplete (`suggestqueries`, `hl=es`, gl = MX/ES/US/CO/AR,
18 seeds). Web search for context. **Not available:** vidIQ (0 credits, resets Dec 15), thumbnails (i.ytimg.com blocked
by the proxy) and transcripts, so "hook" = the description's opening line (usually mirrors the spoken hook, NOT verified)
and the visual format is inferred from titles/descriptions. "rel" = a video's views / that channel's median for its
GTA 6 Shorts aged 2-14 days (374 videos), so channel size cancels out. n < 30 = preliminary.
