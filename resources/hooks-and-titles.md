# Hooks, titles and captions

Templates only. Fill every blank with a fact that was researched for that episode. Never invent a game mechanic, number or location to fit a template.

## Hook lines (segment 1)

| Type | Template |
|---|---|
| Number | "[Number] [thing] in GTA 6. Here's the one that matters." |
| Mistake | "Most players get [thing] wrong in their first hour of GTA 6." |
| Time saver | "This saves you [amount of time] in GTA 6." |
| Before you | "Before you [do common thing] in GTA 6, change this." |
| Hidden | "There's a detail in [area] almost nobody noticed." |
| Comparison | "GTA 5 did [thing] one way. GTA 6 changed it." |
| Countdown | "GTA 6 is [time] away. Do these [number] things first." |
| Verdict | "The reviews are in. Here's the one-sentence verdict." |
| Question | "Is [thing] worth it in GTA 6? Short answer: [yes/no]." |
| Rumour | "Reportedly, [claim]. Here's what we actually know." |

## Titles (main video)

Rules from `CLAUDE.md`: under 60 characters, "GTA 6" first, three options per video.

- GTA 6 [Topic]: [Specific payoff]
- GTA 6 [Number] [Things] Nobody Tells You
- GTA 6 [Topic] Explained in [Time]
- GTA 6 [Thing] vs [Thing]: Which Is Better?
- GTA 6 [Topic]: What Changed From GTA 5

## Short titles and captions

**YouTube Shorts title** (under 60 characters, 1 to 3 hashtags at the end):

    GTA 6 [payoff in 5 to 7 words] #GTA6 #ViceCity

**TikTok caption** (one line plus a question, 3 to 5 hashtags):

    [Payoff in one line]. [Short question]? #gta6 #gtavi #vicecity #gaming

## Thumbnail text

Format from `CLAUDE.md`: `line1` 1 to 2 words, `line2` 2 to 3 words, `tag` 2 to 4 words, and it must not repeat the title.

| Title says | Thumbnail says |
|---|---|
| The topic | The result ("SAVES HOURS") |
| The number | The stakes ("DON'T MISS") |
| The question | The answer ("YES. HERE'S WHY") |
