# Shorts and TikTok playbook

How the `short` block of an episode should be written and posted.

## Shape of a good Short

| Part | Time | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0 to 2 s | States the payoff. The viewer decides here whether to swipe. |
| Proof | 2 to 25 s | One fact, tip or moment per segment, 3 to 5 segments. |
| Turn | last 3 to 5 s | One line that makes people comment or rewatch, then the follow ask. |

- Keep narration under 55 seconds, as `CLAUDE.md` already says. 20 to 40 seconds is the target.
- One idea per Short. If the main video has seven points, the Short takes the single strongest one, not a summary of all seven.
- The first `headline` is on screen during the hook, so it must make sense with the sound off.
- End on a line that loops back into the opening when you can. Rewatches count as watch time.

## Writing rules

- Open on the number, the object or the result: "Up to 212 gigabytes." not "So today we're talking about the download."
- Short sentences. One breath each.
- Say "GTA 6" in the first sentence so auto-captions and search pick it up.
- Ask a question with a short answer ("PS5 or Xbox?"). Short answers get more comments than open questions.
- Label rumours out loud: "reportedly", "according to leaks".
- No story spoilers in the first segment, the headline or the caption.

## Visuals

- Your own gameplay in `media/` beats the generated neon background every time. Put the most striking clip under the hook.
- Keep important action in the middle of the frame. The 9:16 crop cuts the left and right thirds of a 16:9 clip.
- Leave the bottom fifth and the right edge clear. Both apps cover those areas with the caption, buttons and progress bar.
- Captions: 3 to 5 words on screen at a time, high contrast.

## Posting

- Launch week (19 to 26 Nov): post daily at minimum. Search interest for GTA 6 is at its peak, and it will not come back at this level.
- Post the same Short to both platforms, but upload the clean file to each. Never re-upload a download that carries the other app's watermark.
- Write a separate caption for each app (see `hooks-and-titles.md`).
- Reply to the first comments in the first hour. Pin the best one or your own question.

## What to measure

Check these in YouTube Studio and TikTok Analytics after 48 hours, then log the lesson in `content/log.md`.

| Number | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Viewed vs swiped away (YouTube) | Whether the hook works |
| Average percentage viewed | Whether the middle drags. Over 100% means people rewatch. |
| Where the retention graph drops | Which segment to cut next time |
| Comments per 1,000 views | Whether the closing question works |

## Rules that protect the channel

- YouTube limits or removes monetisation for mass-produced, near-identical videos. Every Short needs its own angle.
- Licensed music from the in-game radio can get a clip muted or claimed. Record with the radio off.
- Use only your own gameplay, never Rockstar trailers or other creators' clips.
