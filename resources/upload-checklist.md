# Upload checklist

The machine makes the files. You review and upload. Run this list for every Short.

## Before uploading

- [ ] Watched the whole Short once with sound and once muted
- [ ] First two seconds state the payoff
- [ ] Captions are readable and not hidden by the app's buttons
- [ ] Every fact matches a link in the episode's `sources`
- [ ] Rumours are labelled as rumours
- [ ] No story spoilers in the opening, title or cover
- [ ] No Rockstar trailer footage, logos or other creators' clips
- [ ] No radio music audible under the gameplay

## YouTube Shorts

- [ ] Title under 60 characters, "GTA 6" near the front, 1 to 3 hashtags
- [ ] Description keeps the "not affiliated" line
- [ ] "Related video" set to the main video of the same episode
- [ ] Audience set to "not made for kids"
- [ ] Altered or synthetic content question answered (see below)
- [ ] Pinned comment with the closing question

## TikTok

- [ ] Uploaded the clean file, not a download with a YouTube watermark
- [ ] One-line caption plus a question, 3 to 5 hashtags
- [ ] Cover frame chosen with the headline visible
- [ ] AI-generated content label considered (see below)
- [ ] Music: none added, or a track from TikTok's own library

## AI disclosure

The narration is an AI voice and the backgrounds can be generated, so read each platform's current rule once and apply it the same way every time.

- **YouTube** asks creators to disclose realistic altered or synthetic content. Its guidance has not required disclosure for clearly unrealistic visuals or for AI used as a production aid such as scripts and captions.
- **TikTok** asks creators to label AI-generated content that shows realistic scenes, people or audio.

When in doubt, turn the label on. It does not reduce reach by policy, and it removes the risk of a strike.

## Monetisation thresholds (as I last knew them, confirm before planning around them)

| Programme | Requirement |
|---|---|
| YouTube Partner Programme, early access | 500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in 90 days, and 3 million Shorts views in 90 days or 3,000 watch hours in a year |
| YouTube Partner Programme, ad revenue | 1,000 subscribers, and 10 million Shorts views in 90 days or 4,000 watch hours in a year |
| TikTok Creator Rewards | 10,000 followers, 100,000 views in 30 days, age 18+, and only videos longer than one minute earn |

The TikTok row matters for this repo: the `short` block is under 55 seconds, so those videos will not earn from Creator Rewards. Once the account qualifies, add a 65 to 90 second cut for TikTok.

## Game publisher rules

Rockstar publishes a "Policy on posting copyrighted Rockstar Games material" on its support site. Read it once. In short, it has allowed gameplay videos with your own commentary and has objected to spoilers before release, ripped soundtracks and anything that looks official.

## Music

- YouTube: the YouTube Audio Library is free for YouTube videos.
- TikTok: use TikTok's own sound library, or a track whose licence clearly covers TikTok.
- A track that is cleared for one platform is not automatically cleared for the other.
