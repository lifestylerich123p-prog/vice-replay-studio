# Free credits and free tiers

Figures come from third-party guides dated mid to late 2026, not from the providers. Confirm on each provider's own page before applying.
Most startup programmes expect a registered company, a website and sometimes an investor or accelerator referral. A fan channel on its own may not qualify.

## Free with no application

| What | Free amount | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Kokoro (already in the repo) | Unlimited, local | Daily narration |
| ElevenLabs free tier | Small monthly allowance | Testing a better voice on one Short |
| Cartesia free tier | About 20K credits a month | Testing |
| fal.ai, Replicate | Free tiers on open models | Trying video and voice models |
| Eklipse, Medal.tv, Choppity, OpusClip | Free tiers with limits or watermarks | Testing clippers on your footage |
| YouTube Audio Library | Unlimited | Music for YouTube |

## Startup programmes (application needed)

| Programme | Reported offer | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ElevenLabs startup grant | About 33 million characters over 12 months | Closest fit, for narration |
| Deepgram | Up to $100,000 in credits, 12 months | Speech-to-text and text-to-speech |
| AssemblyAI | Free transcription credits | Transcription |
| Google for Startups Cloud | Up to $200,000, or $350,000 for AI startups, over two years | Third-party models on Vertex AI are not covered |
| Anthropic | Up to $45K in partner credits, more through a participating investor | Works on the first-party Claude API only |
| OpenAI | Sources disagree on whether startup credits still exist | Treat as unconfirmed |

## What actually costs money in this repo

| Cost | How to keep it low |
|---|---|
| Claude usage for the daily run | Short `CLAUDE.md`, few plugins, smaller model for routine runs |
| Voice | Stay on Kokoro until the voice is the limiting factor |
| Rendering | Free, runs on ffmpeg |
| Clipping tools | Start on free tiers, pay for one at most |
