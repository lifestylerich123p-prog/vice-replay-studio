# Topic bank

Angles to use after `content/launch-plan.md` runs out on 23 November. These are angles, not facts.
Each one must be researched fresh on the day, with sources, and checked against `content/log.md` so nothing repeats.

## Graph Shorts (Nov 22: the default Short; our own chart of numbers we checked)

Each angle names the chart and where the numbers come from. Research the numbers fresh on the day (2+ sources),
put the source in the chart `note`, and label estimates.

| Angle | Chart | Numbers from |
|---|---|---|
| A streamer leaderboard (top GTA 6 streams, peak viewers) | bars | YouTube view counts (API), Streams Charts |
| Sales / revenue vs GTA 5 and other launches | counter VS, bars | Take-Two filings, Sensor Tower (estimate) |
| Prices over the years, after inflation | line | launch prices + BLS CPI-U |
| Review scores vs the all-time best | bars (floor 90) | Metacritic, OpenCritic |
| Trailer / video views ranked | bars, counter | Rockstar's YouTube counts (API) |
| How many players finished Chapter 1 / the story | counter, bars | trophy trackers (say "according to trophy trackers") |
| Map size vs GTA 5, RDR2, other open worlds | bars | published measurements (label estimates) |
| Download size / storage vs other games | bars | PlayStation Store, Xbox Store |
| Player counts or search interest over days | line | Google Trends (relative), official numbers |
| Hours to beat vs GTA 5 / RDR2 | bars | HowLongToBeat |

## Fun facts, GTA history and quizzes (Nov 22 evening, owner: "fun facts or history, how much do you know")
Cards only (quiz / text / list / counter). Every fact checked in 2+ sources; no spoilers.
- "How much do you know" quiz Shorts: 3 questions, question card then reveal (template: episodes/gta-history-quiz.json).
  Themes: GTA history (titles, cities, voice actors, release years), GTA 6 launch numbers, Leonida vs real Florida,
  "which GTA came first".
- Fun-fact Shorts: one surprising fact as a `text` card + the proof as a `list` or `counter` ("Vice City was in the
  first GTA, 1997").
- GTA history in numbers: release years on a `line`, sales or review scores as `bars`.
- Instagram knowledge-test carousel (about weekly): cover, 5 questions one per slide (options on their own lines),
  answers on the last slide, "comment your score" (content/carousels/gta-quiz-5.json).

## Guides (steady search traffic; website guides, not Shorts: tips Shorts died, median 237 views)

- First settings to change (camera, controls, audio, accessibility)
- Early-game money, based on what reputable sites and players report
- Fastest ways to get around the map
- Best early vehicles and where players report finding them
- Wanted system: how it works and how to lose the police
- Save system, autosave and what carries over
- Things the game does not explain

## Lists (strong for Shorts)

- Hidden details players found this week
- Callbacks to earlier GTA games
- Small details that show the budget
- Funniest physics moments players are sharing
- Radio stations and what each one plays (describe, do not play the tracks)

## Comparisons

- GTA 6 vs GTA 5: map, driving, police, shooting, each as its own Short
- PS5 vs PS5 Pro vs Xbox Series X vs Series S performance, from published technical tests
- Vice City then and now

## News (only when it is real)

- Patch notes, explained in 60 seconds
- Sales and player-count records, with the source named
- PC version: only confirmed statements, rumours labelled
- GTA Online for GTA 6: only confirmed statements, rumours labelled
- Review scores roundup

## Your own gameplay (the strongest angle)

- "I tried [thing players are talking about]. Here's what happened."
- One chaotic moment, replayed with captions
- A challenge with one rule (for example, a whole mission in one vehicle type)
- Photo-mode tour of one district

| Tue | Graph Short: a comparison with GTA 5 / other games |
| Wed | Graph Short: a trend over time (line chart) |
| Thu | News or patch (graph if there's a number) |
| Fri | Streamer story with our numbers |

| Sun | Weekly recap (the only long video) + its Short |

Guides go to the website every day; they are no longer Shorts.

## Avoid

- Story spoilers in titles, thumbnails or the first 30 seconds
- Invented tips or locations
- The same list re-skinned with a new number
- The same chart layout three Shorts in a row (vary bars / counter / line and where the cutaway goes)
- A question as the first sentence
- Leaks presented as fact
